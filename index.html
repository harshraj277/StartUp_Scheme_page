Enter a few details. This is an eligibility guidance and matching tool — it does not confirm official eligibility. Final eligibility is always determined by the concerned government authority.

How the matcher works: Business Stage, Beneficiary Type, Sector, DPIIT / Udyam, Incubation / Access and Support Type all read directly from the dataset and change your ranking. Funding Required is matched only against the records that state a rupee amount.
